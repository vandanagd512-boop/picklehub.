About PickleHub

Our Story

PickleHub was born from a simple idea: to connect pickle lovers with authentic homemade pickles from local artisans across India. We believe that traditional recipes passed down through generations deserve to be shared and celebrated.

Our platform empowers home-based pickle makers to showcase their craft and reach customers nationwide, while pickle enthusiasts can discover unique, authentic flavors they won't find in stores.

Our Mission

  • Support local artisans and small businesses
  • Preserve traditional pickle-making techniques
  • Provide customers with authentic, high-quality pickles
  • Create a sustainable marketplace for homemade products
